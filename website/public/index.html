A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
We love that.

Here are the answers café owners ask us most.

?
Can BrewPOS work without internet?

Yes. The current Android preview stores sales locally and supports offline checkout. Cloud synchronization across devices is being developed for BrewPOS v2.0.

Can it print both customer and barista tickets?

The current preview supports a customer receipt with prices and a separate barista slip without prices through a compatible Bluetooth ESC/POS printer. Physical printer compatibility needs to be tested with your hardware.

Is the Hybrid Cloud system available now?

Not yet. It is part of the planned v2.0 release. The current Android v1.2 preview is local/offline-first; automatic Supabase synchronization, multi-tablet reconciliation, and the remote portal are still in development.

Does BrewPOS automatically verify GCash or Maya payments?

No. Cash, GCash, Maya and card are presently selectable manual payment labels. Automatic verification or payment-gateway integration has not been released.

What's the difference between Monthly and Lifetime?

Monthly uses a renewable device-based activation. Lifetime is a one-time device-specific license without a fixed monthly expiration. Hardware, support, additional devices, future upgrades and any cloud fees are quoted separately.

Are the plan prices final?

No. The figures shown are proposed, indicative software-only pricing in Philippine pesos, not binding offers. Ask for a formal quote before making a purchase decision.

Is Offline Premium available today?

Not as a fully tested commercial release. Premium features such as staff PIN roles, advanced inventory, enhanced reports and restorable local backups are planned for development and validation.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes, you can inquire about a supervised seven-day trial activation. The private Android preview is intended for testing and should not be used for live sales without backup, hardware, and compliance checks.

Is BrewPOS an official BIR-accredited invoicing system?

No such accreditation is claimed. The preview prints sales receipts marked as not official BIR invoices. Businesses must independently confirm their Philippine invoicing, tax, and registration requirements before using software commercially.
